# Synergy CSP

## Overview

Synergy CSP (Collaboration and Service Platform) is an integrated enterprise collaboration platform designed to centralize document management, project management, workflow automation, and team collaboration.

The platform enables organizations to digitize business processes, improve communication, manage corporate knowledge, and increase operational efficiency through a single integrated environment.

Synergy CSP is designed to support organizations in their digital transformation journey by providing secure, scalable, and collaborative business tools.
