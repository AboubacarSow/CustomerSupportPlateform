# QDMS

## Overview

QDMS (Quality Document Management System) is an integrated Quality Management System (QMS) designed to manage the complete lifecycle of quality-related documents and support organizations in complying with international management standards.

The platform centralizes document management, automates approval workflows, tracks document revisions, manages corrective and preventive actions (CAPA), and provides complete traceability across quality processes.

QDMS enables organizations to maintain regulatory compliance while improving operational efficiency and standardizing quality management practices.

---

# Supported Standards

QDMS supports organizations implementing internationally recognized management systems, including:

- ISO 9001 – Quality Management System (QMS)
- ISO 14001 – Environmental Management System (EMS)
- ISO 45001 – Occupational Health & Safety Management System (OHSMS)
- ISO 27001 – Information Security Management System (ISMS)
- ISO 22000 – Food Safety Management System
- ISO 13485 – Medical Devices Quality Management System
