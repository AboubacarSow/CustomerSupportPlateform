# BEAM (Enterprise Asset & Maintenance Management)

## Overview

BEAM (Enterprise Asset & Maintenance Management) is a comprehensive software platform designed to help organizations manage physical assets, maintenance operations, inventory, procurement, energy consumption, and facility management from a single centralized system.

The platform enables organizations to maximize asset availability, reduce maintenance costs, optimize preventive maintenance, and improve operational efficiency through automation and data-driven decision making.

---

# Business Problems Solved

Organizations often face challenges such as:

- Unexpected equipment failures
- Poor asset visibility
- Manual maintenance planning
- High maintenance costs
- Inefficient spare parts management
- Difficulty tracking work orders
- Energy inefficiency
- Fragmented maintenance records
- Lack of preventive maintenance
- Limited reporting capabilities

BEAM addresses these issues through centralized asset lifecycle management.

# Mobile Application

BEAM Mobile allows technicians to perform maintenance activities directly from mobile devices.

Capabilities include:

- Creating work orders
- Completing work orders
- Viewing maintenance history
- Barcode scanning
- QR code scanning
- Recording labor hours
- Spare part usage
- Inventory lookup
- Offline operation

Offline mode allows field teams to continue working without internet access and synchronize data once connectivity is restored.
