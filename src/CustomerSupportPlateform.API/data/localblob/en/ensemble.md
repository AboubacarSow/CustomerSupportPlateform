# Ensemble

## Overview

Ensemble is a Business Process Management (BPM) and Performance Management platform developed to help organizations design, analyze, improve, and monitor their business processes.

The platform enables organizations to model business processes, define key performance indicators (KPIs), measure organizational performance, manage risks, and align strategic objectives with operational activities.

Ensemble provides a process-oriented management approach that supports continuous improvement and operational excellence.

---

# Business Problems Solved

Organizations often experience challenges such as:

- Undefined or undocumented business processes
- Difficulty measuring performance
- Lack of process standardization
- Poor visibility across departments
- Inconsistent KPI monitoring
- Limited process documentation
- Risk management difficulties
- Inefficient resource planning
- Weak strategic alignment

Ensemble addresses these challenges by providing centralized process management and performance monitoring.
